About

One Dev,
Many Worlds

OnvexaLabs is a solo indie game studio with a simple mission: build games that are genuinely fun, accessible, and worth your time.

From browser-based strategy to mobile puzzles and hyper-casual runners, we keep exploring new genres and mechanics — shipping real, playable titles rather than vaporware.

Built with Godot, three.js, React and whatever tool fits the job — the tech stack doesn't matter, the experience does.
